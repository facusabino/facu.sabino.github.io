About Me

I am Associate Director and Senior Economist at Ipsos UK, where I lead programme evaluation projects using experimental and quasi-experimental designs. I hold a PhD in Health Economics and Policy from Lancaster University, with research focused on health inequities, and bring over 15 years of applied experience in causal inference and evaluation methodology.

My current work focuses on designing and implementing rigorous evaluation frameworks for policy interventions, with particular expertise in randomised controlled trials, difference-in-differences designs, and other causal inference methods to measure real-world programme effectiveness, complemented by process evaluations.
